# A new country edition

Follow `AGENTS.md` at the repository root, step by step; it is the single source of these instructions.
`countries/fr` is a complete worked example.

Before starting, confirm with the person, in one message:
- the edition's own name (not ΑΝΩ ΠΟΛΙΤΕΙΑ; propose a provisional one if they have none);
- the regions people know (NUTS level), and how many cities.

Finish with an honest report: dossiers made, sources read, what remains a draft and what is missing.
